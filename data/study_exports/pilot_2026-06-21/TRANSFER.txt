Pilot study data — CSV bundle (real sessions, not demo)

  questionnaires_wide.csv — 7 post-interaction surveys (all Likert items)
  turns.csv               — 68 dialogue turns
  exit_interviews.csv     — open-ended debrief (Spanish free text)
  runs_summary.csv        — which runs are complete
  sessions.csv            — interaction blocks
  questionnaires_long.csv — one row per survey item
  turns.jsonl             — same as turns.csv + full metadata JSON per line
